# Typecast: Peri pitch script

Peri judges **real-world need, usability and execution**, and requires the business, the person's name and role, and one piece of evidence. Ours: **Mahimn Dave**, filmmaker behind **textbooknotebook**, a student film production, with a voice recording and his exact quotes. Full record: [peri.md](peri.md).

Demo on the **Warehouse handoff** scene ([runbook](demo-runbook.md#scene-texts)). It matches his own example. Start the scout before you talk.

## Two-minute script

| Time | Screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0:00–0:20** Need | Landing page, scout running. | "We built this for **Mahimn Dave**. He's spent the semester making a film with his cast under **textbooknotebook**. He told us directors have a vision in mind, but 'it takes a lot of money, effort, and time to find these exact places because they have to manually go and scout.'" |
| **0:20–0:35** Who it's for | The scene text. | "So the director types the vision the way the script says it. No location team, no driving around first." |
| **0:35–1:00** Usability | Results: one real frame, its aerial, the skeptic note. | "In under a minute: three real buildings, real camera frames from inside their 3D scans, crew parking estimated from aerial images, and a skeptic that argues against each pick. Here, a rail line nearby. Anything we don't know stays marked *unknown*." |
| **1:00–1:30** His feature | Open a warehouse frame → **Edit (AI)** → "old 1980s warehouse". Show the wipe. | "Mahimn's one request: take that warehouse and 'make it more 1980s themed so it helps the director imagine it better.' *(SEE NOTE)* The real walls and trusses stay put, and every AI image is labelled as imagined, not the real room." |
| **1:30–1:45** Follow-up | Type **Need parking for 5 trucks.** | "Plans change: five trucks. Same candidates, re-ranked in seconds, no new search." |
| **1:45–2:00** Execution | Shortlist, LIVE and source labels visible. | "10,568 real camera views across 343 properties in LA and Houston. At least 73% of top-ten results judged relevant on 15 test scenes. In his words: 'very very very helpful for directors.'" |

**NOTE for beat 4:** pick the sentence that matches when he recorded.

- **Recorded after:** "...That's exactly what we'd just shipped, so here it is." Don't say you built it because of him.

## Likely questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| "Is that a real person?" | Show the Instagram and play the recording. |
| "Could he adopt it today?" | Yes for scouting in LA and Houston, where our data is. His shoot location may be outside that, so more cities is next. Don't claim he's used it on his shoot. |
| "Would it save money?" | That's his concern, in his words. We haven't measured savings, so don't give a number. |
| "What's the catch?" | Filming permission, access and truck clearance still need the owner and a site visit. Every pick says so. |
