# Business product decision and reviewer debate

November 15, 2026. Latest GitHub refs were fetched before review; the integrated feature branch includes `origin/dev` `dd9a603`. Buyer, product engineering and evidence/video agents read the actual planner, economics, export and recorded evaluation sources before proposing changes. This is an engineering review, not customer validation or a claim that every external AI service participated.

## Arguments and decisions

| Reviewer | Challenge | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skeptical production buyer | A director can like a room without production accepting access, logistics or costs. A hypothetical reduction in visits does not remove a weekly guarantee. | Separate avoidable cash from labor capacity; preserve assumptions in a producer decision brief. |
| Product engineer | The planner optimizes coverage/location count/visual similarity, not spend. Calling its chosen property “cheapest” is wrong. A visual isochrone or more scene controls would not close this gap. | Keep existing constraint workflow; record selected frame, alternatives, unresolved checks and why the producer wants the next scout. No claim of cost-optimal allocation. |
| Evidence/video reviewer | The commercial minimum is sourced, but three days eliminated is assumed. Repo timing/evaluation is limited and does not measure human time or money saved. | Every factual video figure has a source and scope. Scenarios stay visibly conditional. Prospective actual fields remain blank until production supplies records. |
| Buyer rebuttal | Script search, drive-time filters and PDF exports already exist commercially. A generic memo alone is also not unique. | Differentiation hypothesis: linked exact interior/property/aerial evidence, multi-scene constraint reconciliation, skeptic rejection and reviewed handoff. Validate that combination with a production user. |
| Buyer feasibility objection | Event-only licensed inventory does not establish ongoing company use. Lease availability is not film-bookable inventory. | Post-event data rights/inventory continuity and owner filming permission are explicit pilot prerequisites; do not build or claim a booking marketplace. |

Primary competitor references reviewed: [SetScout](https://www.setscoutapp.com/en/) advertises script/image search, production filters and booking; [Müuse](https://usemuuse.com/) advertises open-web/bookable inventory and physical scouting. These are vendor descriptions, not independently tested capabilities. Typecast does not claim to be the first AI location scout or to outperform their full products.

## Implemented after the debate

1. **Cash versus capacity.** Separate scouting, crew-travel and vehicle commitments: unconfirmed, incremental/avoidable or committed. Unknown commitments keep combined cash impact unknown. Salaried/guaranteed labor can release capacity with zero payroll cash avoided; tool costs remain payable. A cancellable-unit cap models the avoidable part of a guarantee. Negative costs remain negative. Day rates do not imply an invented eight-hour day. Every result remains projected, never measured.
2. **Producer recce decision case.** An allowlisted HTML/JSON artifact binds the selected plan, exact frame/property IDs, alternatives, constraints, source mode, warnings, rate/source status, assumptions, cash/capacity distinction, reviewer and open owner/physical-scout checks. Imports restore assumptions and producer-reported pilot fields only and require a fresh plan; saved financial results cannot become source truth. Optional browser retention has a literal clear/opt-out. Structured home coordinates, source rows and images are omitted.

The prospective record stores baseline/outcome units/costs and source notes. It does not automatically infer causal savings, percentage improvement, verification, a signature or approval. A producer can use it to conduct a real pilot later.

## What was deliberately deferred

Additional image-generation controls, multi-home crew routing, an isochrone, payment/finance integrations, booking and permission automation, and claims of live market prices. These would increase scope while leaving the central question—whether production makes a better, auditable decision—unanswered.

## Business pitch

“Typecast helps a location department decide what to scout next. It connects a script to real interior, property and aerial evidence, tests production constraints, and hands the producer a reviewable decision with explicit cost assumptions and open checks.”

Hardest judge question: **“What billable work disappeared because of your tool?”** Current answer: no customer outcome has been measured. We can show the implemented decision workflow and conditional economics; a prospective producer pilot must establish actual avoidable days, invoices and comparable outcomes.
