## Who to ask (in order of value)

1. A working location scout or location manager.
2. A film student who has scouted or produced a shoot.
3. A film, theatre or video club officer at Princeton or another school at the hackathon.

## Opening (30 seconds)

> We're building Typecast at HackPrinceton. You type a scene, like "1980s police station, night, parking for 3 trucks", and it finds real vacant buildings in LA, shows camera frames from inside their 3D scans, checks if the shot fits the room, and checks crew parking from aerial images. Can I ask you a few questions about how you find locations? About 10 minutes. Is it OK if I quote you on our project page?

## Questions

1. How do you find locations today? Walk me through the last one.
2. How long does it take to get from a script page to a shortlist?
3. What kills a location after you've found one? (Parking, noise, ceiling height, room size, permits, owner says no.)
4. What do you check before you even visit?
5. Would a camera frame from a 3D scan be enough to put a building on a shortlist? What would you still need to see?
6. Is the shot-fit check (lens + room depth) useful, or do you judge that by eye?
7. How do you contact owners today, and what do you ask first?
8. *(Show the demo.)* What's the first thing you'd change?
9. Would you use this on Monday? What would stop you?

Question 8 gives us "one thing we changed". Make the change, then note it above with the commit.
